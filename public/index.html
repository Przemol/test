The Artist

XENO emerged from the streets of Detroit with nothing but a cracked laptop and an unshakeable vision. Blending gritty lyricism with cinematic production, his sound sits at the crossroads of raw emotion and technical precision.

With over 50 million streams across platforms, two sold-out tours, and collaborations with industry legends, XENO is rewriting the rules of modern hip-hop — one bar at a time.
